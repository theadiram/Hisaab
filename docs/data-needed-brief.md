# Hisaab — the data we need

## Our goal

Show **what development was funded, where, for what purpose, how much was approved, who carried it out, who was paid, when, and what completion evidence exists** — from India and state summaries down to individual map dots.

Cover all **28 states and eight Union Territories**, initially **FY 2016–17 through FY 2026–27**. Include Union, state, joint and local-government development, with the funding source clearly identified. Keep identifiable maintenance outside development totals; mark mixed amounts as unresolved.

## Records to collect

| Data                              | What we need                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Funding**                       | Programme/budget head, funding government, grant/loan/channel, Union and state shares, allocated/released/reported-spent amounts, year and reporting cutoff. Keep transfers received separate from development within an area. |
| **Project identity and purpose**  | Official project/activity ID, title, purpose, implementing agency, financial year and state/district/locality identifiers.                                                                                                     |
| **Actual location**               | Official coordinates, road alignment or building footprint, linked to the named project or asset; location precision and source.                                                                                               |
| **Sanction**                      | Approval/order ID, amount, date and revised sanction/amendment records.                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Contract**                      | Tender and agreement/work-order IDs, winning contractor or organisation, contract amount, award date and delivery period.                                                                                                      |
| **Payments**                      | Transaction/bill/voucher IDs, documented project allocation, recipient name and role, gross amount, deductions, net amount, recorded dates and their meanings, status, reversals and reissues.                                 |
| **Completion**                    | Reported progress, inspection records, completion dates and certificates where held. Preserve reported status separately from certificate evidence.                                                                            |
| **Responsible parties and media** | Documented agency, contractor and official roles; verified public photos/logos, site images and geometry where available, with dates and reuse basis.                                                                          |
| **Sources and coverage**          | Original files, URLs, checksums, exact page/table/row references, retrieval dates, units, field definitions, reuse terms and missing records.                                                                                  |
| **Complaint routes**              | Responsible authority, verified official portal/contact, accepted submission method and required applicant details/evidence.                                                                                                   |

## The connection we must prove

**Funding → named project → actual location → contract → bill/payment → recipient → available completion evidence.**

Use official identifiers or documented crosswalks and payment allocations. A similar name, nearby coordinate or matching amount is not enough. One asset can have several projects across different years.

## Rules for collection

- Preserve missing fields as unknown. A historical location alone does not establish spending.
- Keep allocations, releases, expenditure and payments separate; do not count a transfer and its subsequent contractor payment twice.
- Preserve exact source units, revisions, split allocations and failed/reversed transactions. Do not invent payment dates or infer settlement from treasury status.
- Collect public recipient identities and documented roles, not bank accounts, Aadhaar numbers or unnecessary private details. Photos are optional, never guessed.
- Return originals, structured linked records, a coverage/gap list and a restart checkpoint. Records stay private until review approves publication.

**Priority: complete evidence chains for named projects, then repeat the proven method across each jurisdiction — rather than collecting more dots without their money.**
