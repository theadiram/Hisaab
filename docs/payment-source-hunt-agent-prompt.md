Research practical official routes to complete Hisaab's nationwide development-spending map. Each project dot must show what was funded, exact location, purpose, sanction/contract, dated payments, business recipients and available completion evidence, with clickable originals. Prioritize actionable project-to-payment links, not more asset dots or aggregate budgets.

Read `/Users/adiram/Documents/Hisaab/docs/verified-spending-plan.md`, `docs/implementation-status.md`, `docs/nationwide-payment-source-research.md` and current `data/evidence-progress.json` if you have filesystem access. Work read-only in the shared repository; report findings for the implementation agent rather than changing its files or interfering with collectors.

Ten Odisha road finance dossiers have eight differing sanction editions, ambiguous contract units and no verified individual road allocations. 16,768 institution payment rows need bill-to-road allocations and payees. Missing: officially allocated project-level transactions; stable transaction identity; gross/deductions/net definitions and units; business recipient capacity; bank-confirmed status/date and reversals; split-package allocations; contracts and sanction amendments; independently reviewed project geography; available completion certificates; cutoff/completeness and reuse conditions.

For every candidate return: official URL/custodian, access classification, tested report or documented endpoint and method, required filters/auth, exact fields/join keys, actual redacted example if accessible, units/date semantics, geography link, pagination/rate limits, year/state scope, reuse basis, missing fields and exact next assistance required. Preserve originals/checksums privately if tools permit. Distinguish live tested, documented-but-unavailable, indexed lead and failed access. Rank routes by likelihood of obtaining the first defensible card and then scaling.

Do not guess private endpoints, scan ID ranges, bypass CAPTCHA/auth, use leaked credentials, call write/acknowledgement APIs, expose banking/Aadhaar/personal identifiers, send messages or submit RTI/pay fees. Prepare precise custodian/RTI draft requests where necessary. Do not claim universal coverage or infer payments from names, proximity, transfers, sanctions, FTO creation or cumulative expenditure. Deliver a concise evidence-backed findings matrix plus reproducible permitted acquisition instructions and explicit blockers.
