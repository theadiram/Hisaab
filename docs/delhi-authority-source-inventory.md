## Geographic and accounting scope

For a finite Delhi exercise, freeze a period and the **NCT of Delhi boundary**, then include eligible works located wholly in the NCT or an officially documented NCT component of a mixed-location work. Delhi is broader than GNCTD departments and broader than PWD. It includes municipal, cantonment, Union, jointly owned, autonomous and delegated works. A Delhi headquarters, postal address, regional office or project name does not establish NCT geography.

Keep separately: the asset owner; sanctioning/funding authority; procurement buyer; executing division or project unit; payment custodian; contractor; subcontractor where available; and ultimate work location. A loan, equity contribution, grant, inter-agency deposit, land payment, contractor bill and contractor payment are distinct flows. They cannot be added as if they were independent construction expenditure. Decide expressly whether the scope includes repairs, maintenance, surveys/design, enabling works, land acquisition, equipment, utility relocation and capital procurement; do not silently substitute a budget definition of development for a works definition.

Government and publicly funded works form the core ledger. Regulated utility and concession-operator commercial capital expenditure should have a separately labelled scope until the public funding, ownership and payment basis are established. DERC/AERA tariff capitalisation and asset cost are useful discovery or reconciliation evidence, not proof of a vendor's cash receipt.

## Finite strategy for defensible whole-Delhi coverage

1. **Freeze the contract.** Choose the financial year or exact payment interval; retain payment date and report interval separately. Freeze the NCT boundary, eligible work categories, amount basis and treatment of land, maintenance, equipment, consultancy and utility/commercial work. Keep current partial-year and closed-year evidence separate.
2. **Close the authority census from independent official lists.** Reconcile all GNCTD fiscal demands and subordinate/autonomous bodies, PAO/DDOs, the complete GNCTD directory and procurement buyer history; MCD/NDMC/Cantonment and their SPVs; Union ministries/CPWD client-funded works; railway/highway/joint transport agencies; public utility/airport/project SPVs and asset-owner lists. The GNCTD active-tender list is period-sensitive and excludes dormant buyers; its observed rows cannot close this census. Union works require explicit client/asset discovery because all Union capital spending is not routed through CPWD.
3. **Require a finite unit roster per authority.** Capture every division/project unit/DDO and its effective dates, finance custodian, geographic remit, client relationship and reorganisations. Resolve DUSIB jurisdiction changes and the PWD/I&FC PAO cutover. Obtain authoritative “no eligible works/payments” returns where appropriate; absence from a website cannot close a unit.
4. **Obtain complete works and financial registers.** Every unit needs opening/carry-forward contracts/liabilities; in-period works, agreements and bills; cancelled/unpaid/rejected items; advances, deductions, recoveries and reversals; closed contracts and control totals. Cover direct, emergency, nomination, quotation and framework/call-off work as well as public tenders. Reconcile bill and paid registers to official account/control totals and boundary definitions.
5. **Join and allocate from source evidence.** Contract/work ID → invoice/bill → passed amount → paid voucher/payment date → reversals/net basis, with owner/funder/executor relationships. Require actual work geometry/package/chainage and official bill/BOQ/payment allocation for each mixed-location line. Do not allocate by route length, city label or an assumed percentage. Unknown Delhi share stays unresolved and outside the amount total; a documented Delhi-only work can remain a candidate until payment and geometry gates pass.
6. **Publish coverage before aggregates.** Report denominator and examined/complete/pending counts for authorities, units, works and bills, with period and exclusions. Complete coverage can be claimed only for a closed population with reconciled records, including evidenced zero units. Show remaining authority families, inaccessible records and unallocated cross-boundary works explicitly. Source discovery is useful public tracker content; unapproved candidate payment/payee values are not.

Closure is finite because it is tied to the chosen period, boundary, official authority/unit census and complete registers. It is not a promise that an arbitrary list of websites represents all Delhi. The strongest immediate public output is a whole-Delhi **coverage tracker** plus gated work evidence, while records still missing remain visible as gaps.

## Outstanding gaps and limits

- Current complete engineering/paying-unit censuses remain pending for PWD, DJB, I&FC, DUSIB, DDA, CPWD, transport bodies, defence/MES, utilities and institution/estate owners. MCD zones and Revenue districts are complete observed _geographic_ lists, not complete payment populations.
- DDA HQ/field auditable-unit attachments and effective jurisdiction/DDO orders are unusually useful next census originals. Their current versions and contents still need examination.
- NCRTC is an observed public bill/payment reporting route, but no certified report completeness, exact bill-to-agreement foreign key, paid-voucher reconciliation or Delhi allocation has been established. NDMC vendor-detail contents remain unobserved; login/status-service descriptions are not public-payment evidence.
- Rail/highway/metropolitan transport and national utility packages require official NCT splits. Delhi Division, RO Delhi, NCR routes and company-wide accounts are wider than NCT. Concession receipts and corporate financing are different from government vendor payments.
- Hospitals, schools/universities, departmental estates, market committees, central campuses/constitutional estates, project SPVs and delegated builders still require organisation-by-organisation expansion. Listed discovery families must not be rendered as covered authorities.
- This pass used normal public official sources. No email, messaging, application/RTI submission, authentication bypass, automation resumption or publication was performed.
