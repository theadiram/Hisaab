- Public procurement homepages provide current notice samples; historical/current complete registers may require CAPTCHA or other supported access. The Assam active-register CAPTCHA was not bypassed. Source outages and legacy hosts are recorded.
- Goa’s initial budget hostname timed out. An alternative official Goa government budget host and Planning department original were collected; the old failures remain historical gaps.
- Complete original PDFs are preserved even where extraction is capped at 150 pages, a page is scanned/rotated, or legacy fonts need visual/OCR review. Parsed numeric lines/table structures are not reviewed financial facts.
