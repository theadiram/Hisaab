# Hisaab data and map-coverage audit

Audit date: 25 October 2026. Local evidence snapshots mostly date from 5–15 October. This is an audit of what the project already holds, with a limited official-source check; it is not a fresh national data collection.

Follow-up on the same day: [First real project dots: progress and completion path](first-dots-progress-2026-10-25.md) records 21 real project review drafts, fresh official-source recovery and a correction to the initial framing. Complete payment-backed cards are not the only useful output: existing publication rules also support reviewed ongoing projects with payment and completion explicitly unknown. Counts below describe the original audit baseline unless stated otherwise.

## Decision

Hisaab has a large base for an India development atlas, but no defensible percentage of all Indian development projects or development spending yet. The immediate bottleneck is connecting existing records into reviewed project evidence, especially exact geography, bill allocation, payment meaning, recipient role and completion evidence. More disconnected rows will not solve that bottleneck.

The measured holdings below overlap. Do not add them into a total number of projects or payments.

## What is missing for complete dots

A complete financial project card needs a documented connection between funding, an identified work, its actual site/route, sanction, contract or execution arrangement, allocated bill/payment, recipient role and available completion evidence. Active works can have an evidence-rich card with completion explicitly pending; that does not qualify as a completed-work claim. Payment status must retain its source meaning: a cheque or treasury success label alone does not independently prove recipient credit.

For the 536 normalised state project/tender candidates, the field audit finds:

| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------: | ------: |
| Official/source project identity, title and source locator |     536 |       0 |
| Exact project location                                     |       0 |     536 |
| Sanction amount and date                                   |       0 |     536 |
| Contract amount                                            |     198 |     338 |
| Award date                                                 |     186 |     350 |
| Contractor name field                                      |     233 |     303 |
| Project-linked payment amount/date                         |       0 |     536 |
| Completion-certificate source                              |       0 |     536 |

These counts measure explicit normalised fields, not everything that might exist in unprocessed PDFs. A named contractor does not establish that contractor as a payment recipient. Contract/date/recipient fields overlap; do not sum them into nearly complete project counts. All 536 fail the existing full-card field requirements.

The 7,161-road saved audit likewise records zero individual payment evidence and zero certificates; its amount-unit verification is zero. Its stage checks date from 6 October and require source-specific refresh. Of those roads, **5,504 also have district-name proposals, spread across 287 saved LGD districts**. This is a strong review queue, still unapproved.

There are **36,025 unassessed observed PPMS supporting-document links**. Acquiring all would close a document queue, not automatically create paid dots. The saved assessment flags 516 clipped layouts, 420 cheque/report discrepancies and 130 readable beneficiary-total discrepancies; these categories overlap.

## Geographic gaps

The historical map store has no records for **Goa, Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Chandigarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Delhi, Lakshadweep, and Puducherry**. Other datasets do contain records for some of these areas; absence here is specific to this archived map source.

The collected PMGSY response has no partitions for Chandigarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Delhi, or Lakshadweep. Use municipal/PWD/UT procurement and implementing-agency records for them instead of treating scheme absence as zero development.

There are **214 districts without candidate-name match proposals** in the saved registry. First classify each as an identity/rename/boundary mismatch, a source-scope absence or an uncollected source gap. Kerala has 471 PMGSY candidates and 32,154 historical locations but zero current saved district-name proposals: that is a warning against interpreting this metric as geographic absence.

National sector coverage is also unmeasured. Housing, sanitation, railways, health, education and urban works need source/year/agency inventories and project/payment linkages. Historical school or hospital points are not evidence of those sectors' current development spending.

## Recommended sequence

1. **Prove one end-to-end project.** Start with the 155-project Balangir PPMS bundle and existing ten-road dossiers. Choose a record with usable bill arithmetic and recover the actual site, execution/work order, payment amount definitions and allocations, recipient role, transaction status/date and any existing completion certificate. Track source-reported and independently verified stages separately. Review reuse and publication evidence. This proves the collection method, not national coverage.
2. **Create a geographically diverse review queue.** Use the 5,504 geometry-plus-district road candidates across 287 districts and the 184 stronger NHIDCL comparison packets. Keep project cost, contract and paid amounts separate. NHIDCL packets overlap candidate collections and are not additional unique projects.
3. **Reach eight distinct districts.** At least one reviewed complete project per district yields 8/784 = 1.02% district presence on the saved registry. A pilot concentrated in Balangir can produce many cards but still represents only one district. Spread beyond one state where feasible; record sector and fiscal year for every card.
4. **Expand to 40 districts, then 79.** Those thresholds reach at least 5% and 10% district presence. Review source gaps and demographic/geographic bias before presenting the metric as national reach. A district with one card remains largely uncovered.
5. **Build scheme-level completeness before a national completeness claim.** Choose a source, jurisdiction, sector, year and cutoff, establish its official work universe, and publish reviewed projects / total eligible projects plus unmatched records. For spending coverage use reconciled eligible payment amounts / comparable official expenditure, with transfers, reversals and repeated cumulative snapshots handled explicitly.
6. **Use budget/account totals as controls.** Classify the existing Union/state/UT holdings, preserve hierarchies and alternative estimates, then reconcile only comparable scopes. Never make up projects to balance a budget.

## Status discrepancies found

- The older national matrix reports 25,888 state budget candidates. Current state candidate files total **26,965**, matching the current acquisition summary. The difference is **1,077** Goa rows. The older matrix should not drive today's count.
- The NHIDCL prose note describes 211 state candidates / 168 higher-priority records. The later saved comparison packet contains **232 records across 13 jurisdictions**, including three UTs, with **184 contract-facts-first records**. These scopes overlap but differ; use the packet date and scope explicitly.
- The historical map store contains 682 distinct source state/district-ID combinations. This is not a current 682/784 LGD coverage measure.
- Some collections say complete because observed pagination/download traversal finished. That status does not certify complete expenditure, lifetime payments or all source filters.
- Complete-card showcase fixtures are fictional and excluded from coverage.

## Verification and limits

The audit queried the local geography and MPLADS SQLite databases read-only; recounted all 28 state candidate files and all 32 PMGSY raw partitions; checked candidate IDs and geometry proposals; recounted district proposal coverage; and recomputed PPMS observed-document progress. Assertions verify summary consistency and subset associations. Existing source originals and large SQLite files were not all rehashed in this pass.

The anonymous hosted catalogue read attempted on 25 October failed with `fetch failed`. Therefore current hosted publication counts are unconfirmed; zero public works and 86 funding facts are explicitly dated local checkpoints, not live hosted certification. No publication, source collection, database writes or external correspondence occurred.
