# Four problems contributors can help Hisaab solve

Hisaab aims to make public development spending in India easier to investigate: what was funded, where it belongs, what the records say and what evidence of delivery exists. The project is incomplete. Choose the problem that matches your skills; you do not need to work on everything or be a developer.

Join [Hisaab on Discord](https://discord.gg/A4gMtx38z), or propose a focused contribution through GitHub. Please check existing work first.

## 1. Legal protection

**Discord:** `hisaab-legal-protection`

We need a sound foundation for publishing public-interest information and responding when a claim or publication is challenged.

We welcome cited research on applicable Indian laws, publication and reuse rights, contributor protection, complaints and takedown procedures, and sustainable organizational structures. Useful outputs include a source-backed risk assessment, questions for qualified counsel and draft policies for review.

The goal is a legally compliant, defensible and resilient project. Research or a disclaimer does not establish immunity from legal action. Qualified legal review is needed before adopting policies or organizational decisions. Keep sensitive disputes and personal information out of public channels.

## 2. Data collection and verification

**Discord:** `hisaab-data-collection-verification`

This is our largest evidence gap. The retained geography edition contains **8,12,976 historical location records**, not a verified count of current assets, projects or payments. The project-record audit dated **26 October 2026** counts **3,30,080 source-identified project/activity records** across collections. A physical project may occur in more than one collection; this is not a count of unique projects with verified payment data. See `data/project-data-count.json` for its counting rule and source breakdown.

We need help finding official databases, APIs and documents; preparing missing-record and RTI requests; building reproducible acquisition and update tools; matching projects to sanctions, contracts, bills and completion evidence; and identifying duplicates, ambiguity and missing fields.

Useful contributions preserve originals and hashes, record exact source locators and units, explain the linkage method, and distinguish supported claims from unknown or conflicting claims. Project identity, location, payment and completion require their own evidence. No payment is required merely to establish a project identity, and an apparent payment must not be promoted to a verified settlement without supporting records.

The goal is reliable project-level information backed by original records. Collection, publication approval and outbound government requests are separate actions. Discuss the intended custodian and necessary personal details before submitting a request.

## 3. New features and improvements

**Discord:** `hisaab-feature-suggestions`

We welcome features that help people understand records, identify gaps and ask useful questions. Proposals should explain the user problem, evidence needed, technical feasibility, running cost and a testable result.

Possible work includes clearer financial explanations, investigation tools, responsible reporting flows and community evidence review. AI, satellite imagery and geospatial analysis are welcome where their limitations are explicit. Model outputs and imagery interpretations must remain distinguishable from official records and independently established facts.

Start with a small prototype or design. The goal is useful investigation, without unnecessary complexity. A proposal does not authorize a production rollout or a new public claim.

## 4. Mobile usability, accessibility and performance

**Discord:** `hisaab-ui-ux-optimization`

The map, cards and evidence explanations need to work well on phones, tablets and desktops. We welcome usability findings, accessible designs, working prototypes and measured performance improvements.

Focus on map/list navigation, readable financial information, touch targets, keyboard and screen-reader access, loading time and interaction responsiveness. Include the device, reproduction steps and before/after measurements where appropriate. Keep source attribution and evidence limitations visible.

A dedicated mobile app can be explored as a separate proposal with a clear benefit and maintenance plan. Improving the existing responsive website comes first; a native app is not part of the code-cleanup project.

## How to contribute

Share a bounded proposal, research note, design, test case or pull request. Explain the problem, evidence, approach, privacy/reuse concerns and acceptance criteria. Maintainers will discuss practical proposals and review implementation or publication separately.

See [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for setup and review, [SECURITY](../SECURITY.md) for private reporting, and [the data brief](data-needed-brief.md) for required evidence fields. Public progress reports should summarize supported outcomes and remaining gaps without exposing private records or conversations.
