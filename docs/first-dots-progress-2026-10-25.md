## The main correction

The earlier answer treated complete payment-backed cards as the only useful publishable dots. That was too restrictive. Hisaab's existing rules allow reviewed project evidence while payments remain unknown and completion remains pending. The useful first release can show exactly what official records establish, including an archived proposal route or a clearly described source photo location, after review of that narrow claim.

An ongoing project should not be forced to produce a completion certificate. Departmentally executed works should not be forced to produce a winning contractor. Bank settlement must be established before claiming independently confirmed recipient credit, not before acknowledging that an official project exists.

This does not turn a source coordinate into a verified actual site or an unreviewed record into a public project. Project/source identity, geography at its stated precision, district association, reuse and the facts actually shown still need review.

## Completion sequence

1. Take one of the first five Odisha drafts or a single-route road draft through the evidence/source/geography review. Publish only the claims approved by that review. This proves the end-to-end process before scaling.
2. Apply the same decisions to the geographically diverse road batch. The first 8 distinct published districts would reach at least 1% district presence on the saved 784-district register; all 16 would reach 2.04%. This is a target, not achieved coverage. Refresh the district denominator before a current national claim.
3. Expand from the 97-project Odisha bill queue and 195 cross-edition-stable NHIDCL leads. Keep the 11 differing NHIDCL records in reconciliation.
4. Continue the payment/completion evidence tracks separately. Measure project presence, documented approvals/contracts, source-reported payments and certified completion as separate progress counts.

The volume of source data is sufficient to start. Reviewable project geography, clear claim boundaries and the protected publication workflow are the immediate route to useful results. Government-held records still determine how fast full financial chains can be completed; no unsupported completion date is promised.
