# Accepted Funding Atlas phase — 9 October 2026

Union development funding → state/UT → purpose → programme and original evidence.
FY 2016–17 through FY 2026–27. States' own budgets, districts and contractor-payment cards
are subsequent layers. This phase does not establish complete nationwide payments.

## Product contract

Spending opens the atlas with the India-only Mapbox map visible. Default: FY 2026–27,
Allocated, original estimates. Funding and works keep independent year selections.
All 36 current jurisdictions have an accessible searchable list; licensed polygons support
pointer selection. A state opens a desktop side panel or mobile bottom sheet, with
purposes, programme evidence, ministry, channel, grant/loan distinction, Union-share
definition, reporting period, cutoff, publication date, gaps and original page links.
Programme history stays inside the app. Evidence progress and the existing acquisition
and payment reviews remain reachable.

Apple design: restrained typography, immediate press feedback, interruptible spring
panels, clear spatial relationships, reduced motion, increased contrast and opaque
surfaces for reduced transparency. Graduated blue shows recorded amounts. Unknown
amounts are not zero. Colour is never a corruption score.

## Accounting contract

- Allocated, Released and Reported spent are separate measures.
- Original and revised estimates are alternatives.
- Union booked expenditure, including transfers, and recipient utilisation are separate views.
- Grants, capital loans, direct programmes, CSS, Finance Commission development grants
  and disaster funds stay identifiable.
- Maintenance, debt service, general establishment and unrelated expenditure are excluded.
  Mixed development/maintenance amounts remain unresolved until officially split.
- National parents, state children, mirrored releases and overlapping cumulative periods
  cannot be added together. Negative adjustments and recoveries are preserved.
- National totals without a state split stay nationwide. No guessed population allocation.
- Untied tax devolution/general-purpose transfers remain context without invented purposes.
- Historical combined jurisdictions remain combined where no official split exists.
- SDRF/NDRF response, SDMF/NDMF mitigation and identifiable reconstruction are distinct.
  Count only Union share. SDRF is not all Bihar flood money. Do not use an unverified average.
- Allocation or release is never represented as a contractor payment or completed project.

## Acquisition and review sequence

1. Enumerate all eleven annual editions from the official archive. Preserve originals,
   checksums, locators, units, fiscal columns and every acquired demand/row. Existing
   102-demand/408-row context stays immutable and outside the development headline.
2. Inventory and classify individual budget rows: included, excluded, mixed/unresolved,
   awaiting review. Reconcile hierarchy, recoveries and totals before staging additive facts.
3. Collect explicit state allocations/releases from ministry orders, annual reports,
   parliamentary annexures, Finance Commission records and licensed official datasets.
4. Collect actuals and recipient utilisation separately with programme/accounting identities.
5. Prove state accounting with Bihar disaster sources; apply the same report to every
   jurisdiction it covers. Record missing UTs, years and stages.
6. Expand channels/programmes through a source/year ledger. Source access/reuse gates,
   conflicting editions and missing splits remain visible. Prepare custodian requests without
   sending new messages or incurring fees without specific authorization.

Funding facts are separate from work money. Private originals and candidate records are
default-deny. Explicit MFA owner decisions check classifications, source rights, exact units,
jurisdiction, periods/accounting scope and conflicting editions before public projection.
Withdrawal and review invalidate the prior projection. Public service failures return
unavailable, not zero. Source permission is not inferred from public accessibility.

| A    | All acquired demands/programmes accounted for and totals reconciled        | Inventory is not classification or reconciliation completion |

Verification covers exact rupees/lakh/crore, BE/RE alternatives, cumulative and parent/child
duplicates, Union/state share, grants/loans, response/flood distinction, maintenance, missing
versus zero, historical jurisdictions, editions/withdrawal, MFA/privacy, rapid selection,
map fallback and desktop/mobile layouts. Complete interface and complete evidence coverage
are separate claims.
