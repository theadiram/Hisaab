## Official guidance and records to request — 8 October 2026

Three resources linked in the official report footer have been preserved with checksums: [PPMS user manual](https://ppms.odisha.gov.in/upload/ppms_user_manual.docx), [FAQ](https://ppms.odisha.gov.in/upload/ppms_faq.pdf) and [SOP](https://ppms.odisha.gov.in/upload/ppms_sop.docx). They explain workflow; they do not verify an individual pilot payment or grant republication permission.

The SOP's Execution section distinguishes departmental work carried out by selected block staff from tender work carried out by an agency. An AE/JE label therefore needs that project's execution-mode and work-order record before identifying the final recipient. Request the executant selection/order, measurement book, component allocations and onward supplier/labour payment entries where held. Do not relabel every engineering officer as a contractor or automatically approve such transfers.

The SOP's Payment and Disbursement section describes pay orders generated in PPMS and payment files processed through IFMS. The manual's Cashier User—Generate Bill Figure 40-A illustrates a running-account bill with rupee labels, additions, deductions and net payable. This supports asking for distinct gross, deduction and net fields; it does not establish the public IFMS expenditure field's unit/basis or turn the example into project evidence. The eight fractional edition differences remain unresolved pending recorded precision/rounding definitions.

The manual's Case Record Report section (Figures 40-F to 41-B) describes an authenticated Project All Document list and individual/batch downloads. Ask the authorized custodian for that existing document bundle for the named pilot project IDs, including sanction letters, estimates/approvals, work orders, measurements, bills and available handover/completion records. This is an agency export route to request, not a public API or permission to access an official login.

The SOP also contains transition instructions ending 31 March 2025 about hard-copy/portal differences. Request its current effective version and amendments; do not assume dated instructions or delegation thresholds apply to later bills. Downloading these guides provides no new reuse or payment approval.
