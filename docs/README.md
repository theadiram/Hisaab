## Evidence and research

The [data brief](data-needed-brief.md) and [acquisition contract](data-acquisition-contract.md) define evidence requirements. Source-specific findings, collection checkpoints, permission requests and dated audits are research records. Their counts and external-service status belong to the date and scope stated in each document; do not add overlapping totals together or treat a drafted request as sent.

`implementation-status.md`, rebuild/launch status documents and dated execution windows preserve earlier checkpoints. They do not override current architecture, hosting, publication decisions or authorization. Read their dates before following operating instructions. Private incident evidence and recovery credentials remain outside this documentation tree.
