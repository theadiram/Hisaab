**Retrieved:** 15 October 2026. **Status:** private source and transcription; no publication or reuse approval.

The Ministry of Panchayati Raj's official document archive lists a one-page statement titled “Scheme-wise BE/RE/Expenditure for the year 2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25, 2025-26 & BE 2026-27,” dated 4 August 2026. The printed grand totals include the Ministry's Secretariat Services (Non-Scheme) line, so they are not development-only totals. The 2026–27 number is a budget estimate, not spending. The table has individual schemes including RGSA, SVAMITVA and e-Panchayat, but provides no state distribution, district, work ID, bill, recipient, payment date, location or settlement evidence. The 2025–26 revised-estimate cell of ₹772.30 crore is vertically merged across RGSA and Nirbhaya Fund; the amount remains grouped rather than allocated to either scheme. The full visual transcription validates all 13 scheme and grand-total controls; it does not grant publication rights.

This is useful for the national Funding Atlas as an explicitly Union-ministry-wide time series after owner/source-rights review. It cannot be spread across states or attached to project dots. The next acquisition target is the Ministry of Panchayati Raj / Finance Commission state-wise allocation and release statements, followed by official activity-to-voucher and voucher-to-settlement records.

The Ministry describes eGramSwaraj as work-based accounting, with progress reporting, approved cost, administrative sanction, implementing/executing agency, voucher-based digital-signature transactions and cashbook/bank reconciliation. This confirms the relevant records may exist in the platform. It does not establish that all are publicly exportable or that every voucher exposes final bank settlement. [MoPR eGramSwaraj system overview](https://panchayat.gov.in/en/e-gramswaraj/) describes these functions; [MoPR's documents archive](https://panchayat.gov.in/en/documents/) hosts the one-page scheme statement.
